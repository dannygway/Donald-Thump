# Audio brief: Donald Thump

Everything the app needs, with the exact file names. Drop the finished files into `voice/` and `sfx/` and I'll wire them in.

## How to deliver
- **File names exactly as listed**, lowercase. MP3 or WAV both work (if you use WAV, keep the same names with `.wav`).
- **Trim all silence at the start of every file.** The comedy relies on punches landing instantly and on cutting him off mid-word. Leading silence kills both.
- Roughly even loudness across all the voice files, and the same across all the SFX.
- **Keep the voice lines word for word.** The speech bubble shows this exact text while the audio plays. If you change a line, send me the new wording.
- In ElevenLabs, the v3 model's audio tags help with delivery, for example `[shouting] NOT THE HAIR!` or `[nervous] Whoa, whoa.` The tags don't get spoken.
